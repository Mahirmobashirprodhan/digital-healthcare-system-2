📋 Donor Eligibility Criteria

  • ✔ Age: Between 18 and 60 years old.
  • ✔ Weight: Minimum 50 kg (110 lbs).
  • ✔ Interval: At least 3 months (90 days) since your last blood donation.
  • ✔ Hemoglobin: Minimum 12.5 g/dL with normal blood pressure and temperature.
